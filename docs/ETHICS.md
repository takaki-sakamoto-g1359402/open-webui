# Ethics Charter (SITL-Only)

This charter enumerates the ethical expectations for the `swarm_ew` research platform.

- All activities must remain inside research and educational simulators (e.g., Gazebo).
- Hardware deployment, weaponization, intimidation, or surveillance misuse is strictly prohibited.
- Every autonomy decision must be approved by a designated human operator (HITL enforcement).
- Experiments focus solely on non-weaponized early detection objectives that prioritize public safety.
- Contributions must document how safety mitigations (geofencing, rate limiting, logging) are applied.

> _This document is a living artifact and will be refined as the project matures._
