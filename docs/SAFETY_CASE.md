# Safety Case Outline

This outline captures the argument structure for demonstrating SITL-only safety of the `swarm_ew` system.

## Goal
Establish that the swarm early warning platform can be operated responsibly within research simulators under strict human supervision.

## Strategy
- Define operational design domains limited to Gazebo-based simulation environments.
- Allocate responsibilities for human-in-the-loop verification at mission planning and execution time.
- Provide evidence artifacts for geofencing, rate limiting, and audit logging enforcement.

## Evidence (to be collected)
- Configuration snapshots of geofence and safety parameter files.
- Test plans validating fail-safe behaviors during nominal and degraded conditions.
- Review sign-offs by institutional oversight and ethics committees.

_This safety case will be elaborated as implementation progresses._
