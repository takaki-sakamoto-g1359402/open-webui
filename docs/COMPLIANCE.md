# Compliance Checklist (Initial Draft)

The following checklist captures minimum controls required before any SITL simulation may be executed.

- [ ] Confirm the experiment is limited to synthetic data and closed simulation environments.
- [ ] Verify that jurisdictional privacy, aviation, and research governance obligations are satisfied.
- [ ] Document the human approval workflow for mission planning and real-time interventions.
- [ ] Ensure geofencing, velocity limits, and fail-safe behaviors are configured and tested.
- [ ] Review logging, auditing, and retention plans with institutional oversight bodies.

_This template will be formalized with regulatory references in subsequent phases._
